About CampusTrade

CampusTrade was built by students, for students. We make buying and selling second-hand campus items effortless, transparent, and safe.

🎯 Our Mission

To reduce student living costs by facilitating a circular economy of campus essentials.

🛡️ Campus Safety

All sellers are verified campus students to ensure secure face-to-face meetups on campus grounds.
